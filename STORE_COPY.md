# App Store copy — Cove

Positioning, from market research (May 2026): the trusted-private cycle apps (Euki,
Drip, Periodical) are local-only but plain; the polished apps (Flo) were caught
selling data — Meta lost a jury verdict over Flo data in 2025, and Consumer Reports
told users to delete it. The open gap, in users' own words, is **"private like Euki,
polished like Flo."** Lead with privacy + design + one-time price. Don't fearmonger;
state it plainly and let the contrast do the work.

---

## App name
Cove — Private Cycle Tracker

## Subtitle (30 char max)
Private. No account. No cloud.

## Promotional text (170 char max — editable without review)
No account, no cloud, no subscription. Cove keeps every period, symptom and mood
on your device — and turns it into a report you can hand to your doctor.

## Keywords (100 char max, comma-separated, no spaces)

## Description

Most cycle apps live in the cloud. Cove doesn't have one.

There's no account, no email, no sign-up — and nothing to upload, because everything
you log is encrypted and stays on your phone. The only network request Cove ever makes
is to Apple, and only if you choose to unlock the doctor's report. Your logs are never
part of it.

Cove is built for people whose bodies don't fit the textbook — irregular cycles,
endometriosis, PCOS, painful or unpredictable periods. Calm, clear, and deliberately
not pink.

WHAT YOU CAN TRACK
• Period flow, day by day
• Symptoms grouped the way a clinician thinks — pain, physical, skin & hair, metabolic
• Pain by severity (mild / moderate / severe), not vague stars
• Mood, weight, and private notes
• A fast "log a flare" shortcut for bad days

WHAT YOU GET BACK
• A clear cycle summary — length, regularity, and what's worth raising with a doctor
• Symptom patterns mapped against your period
• A clean, formatted PDF report to share at an appointment
• Free CSV export of everything, anytime

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• On-device, encrypted storage
• No account, no analytics, no third-party trackers, no ads
• Nothing is ever sold or shared — there's no one to sell it to

ONE PRICE, ONCE
Cove is free to track with. The shareable PDF report is a single $9.99 unlock —
no subscription, ever. Already purchased? Restore anytime in Settings.

Cove is a record of what you log. It describes your own history and makes no
predictions. It is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis.

## Notes for the listing
- The "$59.5M Flo settlement / delete-your-data" news is the tailwind — the description
  leans on the contrast ("most apps live in the cloud; Cove doesn't") without naming
  competitors.
- "No subscription, ever" is a deliberate differentiator: users resent Flo's ~$10/month.
- Screenshots should show: the calm daily log, the calendar, and — crucially — the
  PDF report (the paid artifact and the strongest "why pay" signal).
- Privacy nutrition label: "Data Not Collected." Keep it that way.
